▾↳ Measuring direction
Draw the axis following light → dark: pale soft earlywood into the dark hard latewood line. That makes ring 1 the oldest ring.
On a two-piece top, trace each half the same way, starting at that half's outer edge and finishing at the centre joint — the youngest wood sits at the joint, the oldest at the edge. Both halves therefore read light → dark; leave both direction settings on that unless the wood plainly says otherwise.
